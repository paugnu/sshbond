## SSHBond Privacy Policy

Effective 6 September 2026. SSHBond is provided by Pablo Iranzo Román (YogaBond). Privacy and support contact: info@yogabond.es.

## Data on your device

SSHBond stores server addresses, usernames, groups, trusted server fingerprints, settings and connection history in its private app storage. Passwords, private keys and saved passphrases use the operating system Keychain or Keystore. SSHBond reads credentials locally to authenticate; private keys are not sent to SSH servers. Optional biometric checks are handled by your operating system; SSHBond does not receive your biometric data.

## Connections you choose

SSHBond connects directly to servers you configure. Those servers receive your network address, username, authentication exchanges, terminal input and any traffic you route through supported SSH tunnels. Password authentication sends your password inside the encrypted SSH connection to the selected server. Server operators control their own logs and data practices. Verify server fingerprints before trusting a new or changed server.

## No developer collection

SSHBond has no account service, advertising, analytics or developer-operated relay. We do not collect or receive your hosts, credentials, terminal contents or connection history. The terminal software is bundled in the app and is not downloaded from a third-party CDN. Apple and Google may process store, installation or crash information under their own policies and your device preferences.

## Sharing and backups

Import, export, clipboard and diagnostic copying happen only when you use those features. Exported SSH configuration can contain server addresses and usernames; review it before sharing. Other apps or recipients can access information you explicitly share. Android app backup is disabled. iOS device backups may include non-secret app files; credentials are stored with device-only Keychain protection.

## Retention and deletion

Data remains until you remove it using the app or operating system. You can remove hosts, keys and trusted fingerprints and clear connection history in SSHBond. Delete saved credentials in the app before uninstalling on iOS because Keychain entries can survive uninstall. Deleting app data does not remove copies you exported or logs kept by your SSH servers.

## Support and changes

If you email support, we receive the information you choose to send and use it to address your request. Never send passwords, private keys or confidential terminal output. Contact info@yogabond.es for access or deletion requests concerning support correspondence. Updates to this policy will appear here and in the public policy with a new effective date.
